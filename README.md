# Senior Research Project

# Evaluating the Effect of Customer Request Clarity on AI Chatbot Intent Recognition and Response Quality

## Project Overview

AI chatbots are widely used in customer service to answer questions and assist customers. However, customers do not always explain what they need clearly. Some requests may be vague, incomplete, or difficult for a chatbot to understand.

This research examines how the clarity of customer requests affects an AI chatbot's ability to recognize user intent and provide relevant, accurate, and useful responses.

## Research Objective

The objective of this project is to evaluate how clear, vague, and incomplete customer requests affect AI chatbot performance. Two AI chatbots will be tested using a fictional restaurant customer service setting.

Their responses will be evaluated based on the following criteria:

- Intent recognition
- Response relevance
- Information accuracy
- Appropriate clarification
- Overall usefulness

## Research Methodology

The project will use a set of customer service scenarios containing clear, vague, and incomplete requests. Two AI chatbots will respond to these scenarios, and their responses will be evaluated using a scoring rubric and a survey.

Each category will receive a score from 1 to 5. The results will be compared to identify differences in chatbot performance when responding to requests with different levels of clarity.

## Expected Outcomes

The research is expected to provide insight into how customer request clarity influences chatbot performance. It may also identify areas where AI chatbots struggle to understand customer intent and where improvements in clarification and response quality are needed.
